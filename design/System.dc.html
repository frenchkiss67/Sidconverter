Reaching this in Tk
Everything here is buildable with ttk.Style on the clam theme, which honours per-element background, bordercolor and padding — the native aqua and vista themes will not recolour and should be left alone. Rounded corners are the one real gap: Tk widgets are rectangular, so the 5 px radius is either dropped or faked with a Canvas. Dropping it costs little — the surface ramp and the borders do most of the work. The waveform, envelope and level meter are all Canvas drawings, which is also the honest way to get the memory map. Fonts fall back to the platform UI face and a monospace stack if Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono are not installed; the layout is sized so that substitution does not break it.
